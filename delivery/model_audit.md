# Model audit: decision scope

The vendor model is unsuitable for compound advancement. Its `vendor_activity_index` was populated after assay results by the vendor QC service (`inputs/legacy/README.md`), and that feature is absent from prospective inventory. It therefore exposes the response to the model, independently of any split score. Removing the column at deployment would not preserve the fitted model. The legacy normalizer also treats censored values as exact targets, changes endpoint names to IC50, mishandles unrecognized units, and clips nonpositive concentrations; mixing assays does not resolve those defects.

We preserve the untouched vendor execution in `model_artifacts/vendor_original_stdout.txt` and `vendor_original_stderr.txt`. The revised diagnostic deliberately uses only the curated exact IC50 labels assigned to CHEMBL5736732, deduplicated by source activity. This is an operationally defined historical assay endpoint, **not** an assertion that this multi-kinase description uniquely identifies CDK2/cyclin E1 at a particular ATP concentration. Its source description lists CDK2/cyclin A2 at 15 µM ATP and CDK2/cyclin E1 at 20 µM ATP, as well as CDK4/6 contexts. Neither historical condition is the proposed matched 1 mM ATP panel.

`analysis/model_audit.py` holds the model specification fixed: an 80-tree random forest, random seed 7, a single execution thread, and Morgan radius-2, 2048-bit binary fingerprints. On one fixed random holdout it compares retrospective index plus descriptors, structure-derived descriptors alone, and fingerprints alone. It then assesses structure-only models with up to five GroupKFold splits, separately grouped by exact canonical isomeric SMILES and nonchiral Bemis–Murcko scaffold. Every held-out prediction and training-set-median baseline is saved. These splits protect against exact-chemistry reuse and against scaffold reuse respectively; the latter can still share substantial chemical substructure. No hyperparameter search, test-driven model choice, candidate forecast, or calibrated uncertainty interval is produced.

Execution completed successfully under the approved single-core lease. The untouched vendor script reported **R² 0.960186 and MAE 0.066899** across 262 accepted rows. Its random train/test split shares nine canonical chemicals and source activity 28327046; 49 targets are nonexact, two endpoint types (IC50 and Ki) and four assay IDs are pooled. These are observed split defects, in addition to the decisive retrospective-feature leakage.

After curation there are **122 rows, 117 exact canonical chemical groups and 24 Murcko scaffolds**. The index correlates 0.999609 with the corrected label (MAE 0.012337 log units). On the *same* 31-row random holdout, including the index gives R² 0.989192 and MAE 0.034150; descriptors alone give R² 0.329573 / MAE 0.362443; fingerprints alone give R² 0.370149 / MAE 0.331191. That curated random split still shares one canonical chemical and is therefore only an ablation diagnostic.

The structure-only model has modest historical signal relative to its baseline, but the scaffold result is uneven. Test fold sizes are 66, 22, 12, 11 and 11; four of five within-fold R² values are zero or negative, and one fold's MAE is worse than its training-median baseline. The pooled positive R² must not be presented as uniform ranking performance. Median nearest-training Tanimoto is 0.855163 in chemical-group CV and 0.794649 in scaffold CV; the latter still tests analogs within a related chemical family. The minimum is 0.604938 for both protocols. CED-23 and CED-24 lie far outside even that tested domain (maximum similarity 0.191919 and 0.200000). These observations justify abstention, not an arbitrary similarity cutoff with claimed coverage.

Six inventory structures (CED-17 through CED-22) exactly match eligible historical training structures; their corresponding measurements are observations, not a model's independent successes. `inventory_applicability.csv` reports structural matching only: valid SMILES does not validate a vial's claimed identity, and STOCK-28 remains an identity conflict adjudicated by the main curation. The split metrics weight activity rows; repeated genuine measurements of one chemical are held in the same fold but retained as separate responses. All modeled rows belong to the same source document, so independent study/time validation is unavailable. R² and MAE here describe historical holdouts only. Fold heterogeneity, finite data, shared chemistry and common source document restrict interpretation; a pooled score does not establish prospective coverage. Out-of-fold errors are not a justified uncertainty interval for cellular target engagement, CDK1 selectivity, or compounds outside the historical domain.

Practical role: use chemical similarity to inspect whether an inventory structure is represented by historical evidence, and historical observed labels as context with their actual assay attribution. Measure missing cellular engagement and establish a matched biochemical CDK2/CDK1 panel before treating compounds as selective. Do not rank the present shortlist using the vendor score. CED-23/24 are a different chemical series; similarity can flag extrapolation but cannot infer their target engagement.
