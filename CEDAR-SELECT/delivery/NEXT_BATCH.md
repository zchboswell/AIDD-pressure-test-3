# Project findings to carry forward

For this spiro-lactam study, optimize the paired change in CDK1/CDK2 ratio, not CDK2 potency alone. S2→S3 improves both enzyme potencies almost equally; extra fluorination does not establish better discrimination. Retain same-plate direct parents and predeclare what gain justifies the edit. Reassess this lesson with genuinely independent matched data; it is not a universal fluorination rule.

Keep all source leakage relationships as well as graph identity in validation groups. Here an isotopologue differs in graph text but is explicitly linked to its parent. Stereo-only or canonical-graph-only splitting misses that relation. Verify the transitive union before fitting. The final curation retains both raw IDs and validation-group keys.

Censored off-target labels are central decision evidence. Excluding them from exact regression without checking their inequalities produces optimistic CDK1 potency forecasts. One-sided loss was tested and did not solve held-out failure; do not treat its fit convergence as scientific validation. Future work needs actual off-target concentration-response coverage, independently qualified assays and uncertainty, not more rounds of tuning this small exposed set.

A robust protein-loop displacement does not make a rigid transplant a robust selectivity test. Distinct-ligand snapshots and alignment sensitivity must be separated from an energetic mechanism. Reassess when the same new compound has qualified complexes or orthogonal binding evidence for both targets.
