## Executed predictive contribution

Question: can nearby unmeasured compounds in this one chemical study be usefully ranked for CDK2 potency or CDK1/CDK2 discrimination? This is narrower than another-study or cellular transfer. We fixed Morgan radius2/2048-bit chiral fingerprints, Tanimoto kernel-ridge alpha1, three-nearest-neighbor similarity weights, and median baselines before fitting. No hyperparameter search, descriptor selection or tuning on development labels was used. All supplied development labels are known-exposure labels, not a blind test.

Five-fold GroupKFold uses the49 training compounds; grouping unions supplied leakage-group IDs with stereo-stripped connectivity so stereoisomers and source-linked isotopologues stay together. Both target measurements of each molecule remain together. The11 development compounds remain separate and have no union-group overlap. Train partitions contain49 exact CDK2,36 exact CDK1; development11 and9 respectively. The15 censored CDK1 outcomes are excluded only from exact regression, retained in coverage and bound checks. Models for delta p use exact pairs directly; they do not mix simulated cellular outcomes or unrelated ChEMBL contexts.

All exact held-out rows/predictions, neighbor similarities and metrics are supplied. For kernel-ridge development delta p, bootstrap MAE range .167–.301 from2000 resamples is descriptive uncertainty over these nine fixed prediction errors, not a prediction interval or independent-study calibration. Spearman is .20. Small label spread permits modest MAE with negative R²; choosing a tiny MAE improvement over median as proof of useful ranking would be misleading. Training pooled folds also differ in medians, so pooled median-baseline correlation is not zero by construction. The development sample is too small and correlated to establish a stable winner.

A consequential limitation is endpoint-dependent censoring. The exact-only CDK1 model predicts stronger binding than all13 training-CV and2 development upper pIC50 bounds allow. The one-sided squared-hinge kernel sensitivity fit penalizes only predictions above a CDK1 bound, and varies relative bound penalty .25/1/4 at fixed regularization. All18 optimizer fits converge. At weight1, development CDK1 MAE improves to.186 and derived delta MAE is.200, but both development bounds and all13 training-CV bounds are still violated. Raising weight worsens other errors without solving held-out censoring. This is not a censored likelihood model, and non-identifiability below the assay threshold persists. The result argues against optimistic off-target forecasts, not against retaining censored compounds as potentially valuable experiments.

Model purpose was falsifiable: if stable discrimination ranking beat simple baselines and respected bounds, it could prioritize synthesis. It did not. We therefore choose matched edits for information and abstain from new potency/window forecasts. `predictions.csv` records explicit abstentions and nearest-neighbor descriptions. D1/D2 similarities≈.97 illustrate why fingerprint proximity alone is inadequate: local radius fingerprints weakly distinguish this ring expansion, while observed ring-size changes affect potency. No similarity cutoff is calibrated for novel designs. No full A1 or hidden-result score is claimed.

Quantitative ratios and source pointers are in `analogue_pairs.csv` and the chemistry contribution. Two ring-expanded methyl/methoxy precedents motivate a small2×2 prospective comparison; one CDK1 bound prevents a claim of monotonic benefit. The new structural work goes beyond the predecessor's coordinate-frame audit. It compares supplied deposited CDK2 and CDK1 complexes, maps ligand atoms through chemical-component records, examines ligand/pocket quality, measures contacts and tests sensitivity to chain and protein fitting choices. The complexes bind different ligands, so observed Tyr15 displacement and steric-transplant sensitivity cannot isolate intrinsic target selectivity or validate a spiro pose. Retain source resolution/validation limitations and do not interpret proximity as a free-energy calculation. No docking/MD/free-energy calculation was warranted by an unvalidated binding mode and confounded receptor comparison; this decision follows the executed sensitivity test.

Public primary search expanded the mechanism/assay perspective without pooling labels. The INX-315 study reports intracellular CDK2/E1 and CDK1/B1 engagement of2.3 and374nM and identifies off-target risks; these are external assay observations supporting an orthogonal reference strategy, not performance targets predicted for D1/D2. ## Prospective experiment interpretation

`experiment_plan.csv`, `tranche_budget.csv`, and `plan_validation.json` define a cost-ceiling proposal, not quotes or executed orders. Three independent day/preparation replicates of all four core compounds on shared days16–21 give12 paired sets (24 primary curves), plus orthogonal/ATP-shift checks. Fit log IC50s with curve QC; report each replicate and paired-day log-ratio differences. Extend concentration ranges only when measured solubility and interference permit. Predeclare a≥.5-log ratio-gain margin over parent; ideally its paired confidence interval excludes zero and all three days meet direction. Three days do not provide precise population variance; if inconclusive, report uncertainty and retain funds rather than manufacture significance. Global biochemical preferences remain≤100nM CDK2 and≥10-fold ratio.

Cellular studies explicitly include both CDK2 and CDK1 engagement, a CDK9 countercheck, exposure/concentration controls, and phenotype/dependency controls. Cellular engagement ratio remains an assay result, not a therapeutic safety margin. CCNE1 alone is not sufficient for sensitivity; RB status, compensation and existing isogenic/genetic controls must be documented. A new engineered panel would exceed this conditional schedule. Early HLM/solubility measurements rank exposure risk; they do not establish pharmacokinetics.

## Limitations and reproducibility

The contribution establishes a reconciled evidence trail, unsuccessful but informative within-study predictive tests, structural sensitivity analysis, precisely specified prospective chemistry, and a budgeted decision experiment. It does not establish a new measured lead, external generalization, improved selectivity outside the fitted study, cellular safety, in-vivo efficacy, actual supplier stock or guaranteed30-day synthesis. Model failure does not excuse omitted chemistry: two exact designs and their feasible conditional routes are supplied. Replays and checks are documented in `REPRODUCE.md`; resources and sources are in `evidence/`.
